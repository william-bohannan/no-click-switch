## Short description

Always-on-top Windows top bar. One tab per open window. Hover to switch — no click.

## Description

No Click Switch (NCS) is a thin always-on-top bar at the top of your screen. Every open window gets a tab. Hover a tab to bring that window forward and fill the space under the bar. No click required.

Replace the Windows taskbar while you work, or run both. The bar stays on top, tabs wrap if you have many windows, and the active window is quietly highlighted.

**Switch without clicking**
Hover a tab to focus that window. Click does the same. Right-click a tab to pin, minimize, or close.

**Made for real desktops**
One bar per monitor by default, or a single bar on the primary display. Drag tabs to reorder. Pin favorite apps so they stay at the front of the strip.

**Glanceable stats**
Optional CPU and memory %, disk use, CPU/GPU temperature (Windows thermal APIs and nvidia-smi), and a compact clock.

**Your bar, your rules**
Light, dark, or system theme. Opacity and Mica/Acrylic blur. Hover delay, tab width, auto-hide, exclude list, and optional Ctrl+Alt+1–0 (or Win+1–0) hotkeys.

**Stay out of the way**
A tray icon can hide the bar instead of quitting. Opening the bar turns on Windows taskbar auto-hide; closing it restores the taskbar.

Updates come from the Microsoft Store. Uninstall from Settings → Apps. The app starts with Windows (turn that off in Settings → Apps → Startup).
